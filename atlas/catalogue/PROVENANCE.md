# Historical catalogue provenance

This catalogue contains dated source descriptions and exact capture bindings, not promises of current availability or functionality. The historical review fields are preserved. They do not describe a new review at packaging time.

The release scope does not require a complete retrospective evidence archive. Some original source extracts, supplements and review bodies are currently unavailable. Historical ledger/component files and exact captures are available, but do not replace missing originals. Full reproduction of every historical editorial review is therefore not claimed. The archive-completeness status remains unresolved, not passed.

The files in `provenance/` preserve historical review lineage, source URLs, dates, image hashes, component roles and known limitations. Dependency identifiers describe the historical review basis. They are not an assertion that all underlying bodies are available and are not a separate mandatory release archive. The historical `component_dependencies_required` fields in overview checks retain their original meaning within those reviews; they do not impose a complete retrospective archive as a release condition under this packaging scope.

Available originals remain unchanged outside this public package. No missing body is reconstructed, no old visit is redated and no claim of whole-card re-review is added. Public readers cannot independently reproduce missing historical source-review bodies from these projections.

## Limits that remain

- Source and capture dates are historical, not continuous service monitoring.
- Source-description and capture checks are not functionality, payout, safety, operator-identity or private-data audits.
- Source-specific caveats, including incomplete and corrected source captures, remain part of the historical record. The projections do not turn an earlier incomplete source into a complete one.
- Memecorp's historical Google Safe Browsing no_data (code 6) remains distinct from the three clean lists and from a four-provider clean result.
- Botnet's original integration checker failed the image byte budget. The separately reviewed derivative and wired integration close only that later image issue, without inventing a new visit or capture.
- The 18 bounded notes are not accepted overviews or permanent closure decisions. Closed history is not current service availability.
- Exact image/source/time correspondence, safety behavior, secure private-input checks, actual CI and same-commit staging remain technical release requirements. Local success is not permission to publish, merge or deploy.
